## How to read the counts

- **High count** → load-bearing. It gets a token named for its **role**, and the
  sweep repoints every use.
- **Count of 1–2 beside a high-count near-twin** → almost always a typo or a
  one-off. `RGBA(241,46,76)` × 1 next to `RGBA(241,46,73)` × 40 is a typo. Fold it.
- **Count of 1, no twin** → decide deliberately. Either it earns a token, or it is
  drift to delete. Do not tokenize everything; a token per value is the same
  problem with extra indirection.
